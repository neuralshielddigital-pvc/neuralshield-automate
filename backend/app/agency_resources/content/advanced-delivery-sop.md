# Client Delivery SOP

## 1. Handoff
- Signed scope recorded.
- Commercial status confirmed.
- Primary contacts confirmed.
- Required access inventoried.

## 2. Kickoff
- Reconfirm objective.
- Reconfirm scope and exclusions.
- Confirm owners and response expectations.
- Confirm reporting cadence.

## 3. Baseline
Capture current metrics before changes are made.

## 4. Delivery
Every work item should have:
- owner,
- due date,
- acceptance condition,
- evidence,
- status.

## 5. Quality gate
No deliverable is marked complete until its acceptance condition is verified.

## 6. Client communication
Report:
- completed,
- in progress,
- blocked,
- decision needed,
- next milestone.

## 7. Close / renew
Compare final state with baseline, document residual risks and propose the next commercially justified phase.
