# Commercial-Use Operations Guide

This resource is operational guidance only. Commercial use remains subject to the licence terms published by NeuralShield Digital.

## Before client use
- Confirm the asset is included in your purchased entitlement.
- Do not represent NeuralShield intellectual property as your own proprietary work where the licence does not permit it.
- Do not redistribute source assets as standalone resale products unless the published licence explicitly permits that use.
- Keep a record of which client engagement uses which eligible asset.
- Remove NeuralShield confidential or account-specific information before client delivery.

## Recommended internal register
Track:
- client,
- engagement,
- asset used,
- date first used,
- internal owner,
- applicable licence version.

For legal interpretation, rely on the published licence/terms rather than this operational guide.
