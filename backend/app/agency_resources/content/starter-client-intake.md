# Client Intake Checklist

Use this checklist before accepting a new agency engagement.

## Commercial fit
- Confirm the client's primary business objective.
- Confirm the service requested and expected outcome.
- Confirm decision-maker and day-to-day contact.
- Confirm realistic budget range.
- Confirm target start date and urgency.
- Confirm procurement, legal or security requirements.

## Delivery inputs
- Current website and key landing pages.
- Existing analytics and reporting access.
- Brand guidelines and approved messaging.
- Existing campaigns, assets and historical results.
- Primary competitors.
- Existing CRM or lead-management workflow.
- Internal approval process.

## Success definition
Record:
1. baseline metric,
2. target metric,
3. reporting period,
4. owner,
5. measurement source.

Do not start delivery until ownership, scope and success criteria are explicit.
