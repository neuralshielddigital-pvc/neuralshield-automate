# Client Health Review

Score each area 1–5.

## Commercial health
- Client sees measurable value.
- Scope remains commercially viable.
- Payment status is healthy.

## Delivery health
- Milestones are on time.
- Dependencies are controlled.
- Quality issues are low.

## Relationship health
- Decision-maker remains engaged.
- Communication is timely.
- Expectations are aligned.

## Growth / retention
- Next business problem is identified.
- Renewal timing is known.
- Expansion is justified by evidence.

Any score of 2 or below requires an owner, corrective action and due date.
