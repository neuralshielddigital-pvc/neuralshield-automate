# Starter Toolkit: Start Here

NeuralShieldDigital Agency Client Engine

Starter Toolkit is a $27 one-time collection of agency workflow planning resources.
The download contains 37 source files in PDF, Word, Excel and HTML formats, plus
this guide and a contents list. Several documents are supplied in two formats;
37 files does not mean 37 different tools.

## Download and open

Download **Starter Toolkit Download Pack** from your member portal and extract
the ZIP on your computer. Start with the PDF guides. Use the Word and Excel
versions when you want to fill in or adapt a worksheet. HTML files open locally
in your browser. The separate intake, discovery and pipeline templates remain
available in the portal.

## Suggested order

1. Complete **15 Minute Quick Start** to choose one repeated process.
2. Use **Workflow Readiness Scorecard**, **Current Process Map** and
   **First Workflow Selection and Approval Planner** to define its scope.
3. Use the niche, ideal-client and qualification worksheets to assess fit.
4. Use the pilot offer, proposal, statement of work, access checklist and asset
   register to plan onboarding. These are templates for you to complete.
5. Follow **Setup and Controlled Pilot Guide** and **30 Day Implementation Plan**.
6. Use the FAQ and troubleshooting runbook when you need help.

## Included scope

This Starter package supplies planning worksheets, editable templates and written
guides. Some guides describe a broader implementation process and refer to
additional workflow specifications, operating logs or training. Only the files
listed in CONTENTS.txt are included in this download. The package does not include
recorded videos, a five-workflow vault, done-for-you implementation, Pro
Communications, Advanced Operations or the Agency Commercial License.

A NeuralShieldDigital Workflow Automation subscription is separate:

Purchasing this toolkit does not activate a software subscription or integrations.
