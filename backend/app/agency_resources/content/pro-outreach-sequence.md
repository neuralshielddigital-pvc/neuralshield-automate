# Agency Outreach Sequence

Use only with appropriately sourced business contacts and respect opt-out requests.

## Touch 1 — relevance
State:
- why the company was selected,
- one observable business problem,
- one specific outcome your service addresses,
- one low-friction question.

## Touch 2 — useful evidence
Send a short diagnostic observation, benchmark, checklist or example.
Do not repeat the first message.

## Touch 3 — operational angle
Explain one workflow, revenue or execution cost that may be reduced.

## Touch 4 — decision
Ask whether the topic is:
- relevant now,
- relevant later,
- or not relevant.

Stop the sequence when the recipient declines or opts out.
