# Discovery Questionnaire

## Business
1. What result matters most in the next 90 days?
2. Why is this priority important now?
3. What is the commercial value of solving it?
4. What has already been attempted?

## Customer
5. Who is the highest-value customer segment?
6. What problem causes that customer to act?
7. What objections delay purchase?
8. Which proof points build trust fastest?

## Acquisition
9. Which channels currently generate qualified demand?
10. What happens after a new lead arrives?
11. Where are leads currently being lost?
12. What is the current response time?

## Delivery
13. Who approves messaging and creative?
14. Which systems must integrate?
15. Which reporting metrics are mandatory?
16. What would make the engagement unsuccessful?

End the call with a written problem statement and one measurable target.
